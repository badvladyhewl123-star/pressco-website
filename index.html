Frequently asked questions

A few things to know.

Here are some helpful details before you request your quotation.

How can I request a quotation?

Complete the inquiry form with your product, quantity, size, paper preference, deadline, and design details. The form will prepare an email for you to review.

Are the prices displayed final?

No. The displayed prices are sample starting prices for this website prototype. Actual prices should be confirmed based on your selected specifications and costs.

Can I request a custom design?

You can describe your preferred design in the inquiry form. Design availability, artwork preparation, and any additional fees must be confirmed before ordering.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The quantity depends on the product and printing specifications. Include your desired quantity in the form so it can be reviewed for your quotation.

How long will printing take?

Turnaround time depends on the product, quantity, design approval, and production arrangements. Include your target deadline so it can be considered in the quote.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery or pickup options have not yet been confirmed for this prototype. Add your preferred arrangement to your inquiry for discussion.
