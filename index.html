Frequently asked questions

A few things to know.

Helpful answers before you send your request.

How can I request a quotation?

Build your order, review the summary, and submit your request. Your email application will open with the details for you to review and send.

Can I use my own design?

Yes. Choose Upload My Design and select your artwork file. Attach the file to your email before sending your inquiry.

Can I ask for a custom design?

Yes. Choose Request a Custom Design and describe your preferred style, colors, wording, and overall look in the design field.

How are printing prices calculated?

The estimate is based on the selected product, quantity, size, paper, printing sides, and finishing options. The final quotation is confirmed after the order details are reviewed.

Can I choose delivery or local pickup?

Yes. Select your preferred fulfillment method in the order builder and include your preferred date and schedule.
