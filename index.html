A little about us

PressCo. is a modern custom paper-products studio built around the idea that good printing should feel creative, personal, and accessible.

Our story

Inspired by the idea of presko.

PressCo. brings together professional printing solutions and a clean, contemporary visual identity. Our brand takes inspiration from the Filipino concept of “presko” — fresh, light, and refreshing.

We aim to help students, entrepreneurs, small businesses, and individuals turn their ideas into printed materials that communicate clearly and leave a lasting impression.

From business essentials to personalized paper products, every print begins with an idea worth sharing.
